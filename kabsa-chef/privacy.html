Kabsa Chef privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Kabsa Chef (شيف الكبسة, app id com.petpatch.kabsachef) collects, why, and what choices you have. The game is published by Pet Patch Oy.

Who we are

Pet Patch Oy (business ID 3470744-2), Espoo, Finland, is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: hello@ykiexam.com.

No account needed

You play without an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress is saved on your device only.

What the game collects

  • Game analytics (Google Firebase Analytics): an app instance ID, device model, operating system, language, country (from your IP address), app version, and what happens in the game, such as levels reached, buildings bought, ads watched and purchases made. We use it to understand how the game is played and to improve it.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): technical details of a crash, such as the device, the app version and where in the code it happened. We use them to fix bugs.
  • Game settings and tests (Firebase Remote Config and A/B Testing): an app instance ID, so we can change game settings without an update and compare versions of the game.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): the game shows ads. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing): Google handles the payment. We receive only the order details (product, price, currency and order ID), never your card or payment details.
  • Notifications: reminders (for example "your gift is ready") are scheduled on your device. No data is sent for them.

Legal basis

We process analytics and crash data based on our legitimate interest in running and improving the game. Personalised ads are based on your consent where the law requires it. Purchase data is processed to provide what you bought.

Who receives the data

Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play) processes the data for us. Its data may be processed outside the EU, including in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Analytics and crash data are kept for at most 14 months and then deleted. The save game stays on your device until you delete the game or its data.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Change your ad consent choice at any time from the game's settings.
  • Deleting the game removes your progress from the device.
  • You can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Write to hello@ykiexam.com and include the app instance ID if you can. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Finland: the Data Protection Ombudsman, tietosuoja.fi).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.

ما الذي تجمعه اللعبة

  • تحليلات اللعبة (Google Firebase Analytics): معرّف للتطبيق على جهازك، وطراز الجهاز، والنظام، واللغة، والدولة (من عنوان IP)، وإصدار اللعبة، وما يحدث داخل اللعبة مثل المستويات والمباني والإعلانات والمشتريات. نستخدمها لفهم طريقة اللعب وتحسين اللعبة.

  • إعدادات اللعبة والتجارب (Firebase Remote Config و A/B Testing): لتغيير إعدادات اللعبة بدون تحديث ومقارنة نسخ مختلفة منها.
  • الإعلانات (Google AdMob): تعرض اللعبة إعلانات. قد تجمع Google معرّف الإعلانات في جهازك وعنوان IP ومعلومات الجهاز وتفاعلك مع الإعلانات لعرضها وقياسها ومنع الاحتيال. حيث يفرض القانون ذلك (مثل الاتحاد الأوروبي والمملكة المتحدة) تطلب اللعبة موافقتك قبل تخصيص الإعلانات.
  • المشتريات (Google Play Billing): تتولى Google عملية الدفع. نستلم تفاصيل الطلب فقط (المنتج والسعر والعملة ورقم الطلب)، ولا نرى بيانات بطاقتك أبداً.
  • الإشعارات: التذكيرات تُجدول على جهازك ولا تُرسل أي بيانات.

من يستلم البيانات

تعالج Google (Firebase و AdMob و Google Play) البيانات لصالحنا، وقد تتم المعالجة خارج الاتحاد الأوروبي بما في ذلك الولايات المتحدة. راجع سياسة خصوصية Google. لا نبيع بياناتك.
